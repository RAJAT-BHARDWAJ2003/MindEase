About Mental Health

Mental health includes our emotional, psychological, and social well-being. It affects how we think, feel, and act. It also helps determine how we handle stress, relate to others, and make choices.

Mental health is important at every stage of life, from childhood through adulthood. Understanding mental health and recognizing the signs of mental illness can help you seek support early.

Common mental health disorders include depression, anxiety, bipolar disorder, and schizophrenia. With proper care, many people recover or manage their conditions effectively.
